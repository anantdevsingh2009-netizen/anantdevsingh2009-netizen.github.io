MY PERSPECTIVE

What web designing means to me

Web designing is not only about making a beautiful page. It is about understanding how a computer works, organising information, solving problems and creating something useful for people.

My goal is to start from zero, understand every step and slowly turn knowledge into real websites and creative digital projects.
